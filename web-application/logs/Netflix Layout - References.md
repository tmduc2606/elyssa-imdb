****Netflix Landing Page Structure Summary****  
__Designed for AI Agents (Text-Only Parsing Context)__

This landing page is a conversion-focused, single-page application designed to drive free trial sign-ups. Its structure moves from a primary Call to Action (CTA) down through content showcases, value proposition cards, an FAQ section, and finally, legal/corporate navigation.

### 1\. Global Top Banner & Header

-   ****Promotional Top Bar:**** A distinct, thin colored banner at the absolute top, featuring a gift box icon and text promoting a free trial (e.g., "New to Netflix? Try 30 days for 0 ₫").
-   ****Main Navigation Bar (Sticky/Floating):****
-   -   ****Branding:**** Netflix logo (Red text, top left).
    -   ****User Utility (Top Right):****
    -   -   ****Language Selector:**** A dropdown menu containing a globe icon and current language text (e.g., "English").
        -   ****Authentication:**** A prominent "Sign In" button (Red color).

### 2\. Hero Section (The Main Banner)

-   ****Visual Background:**** A large, dynamic collage of movie and television show posters with a dark overlay to make text legible.
-   ****Primary Value Proposition (Center Content):****
-   -   ****Headline:**** Large, bold, white text stating "Unlimited films, series and more."
    -   ****Sub-headline:**** Slightly smaller white text stating pricing and policy (e.g., "Plans start at 74,000 ₫. Cancel at any time.")
    -   ****CTA Prompt:**** Smaller, centered text asking the user to act: "Ready to watch? Enter your email address to start your membership."
-   ****Lead Capture Form:**** A primary conversion element consisting of:
-   -   ****Input Field:**** A text box labeled "Email address".
    -   ****Submit Button:**** A large, red action button adjacent to the input field reading "Try 30 days for 0 ₫ >".
    -   ****Micro Copy:**** Fine print immediately beneath the button stating "New members only. Terms below."

### 3\. Content Showcase & Value Carousels (Mid-Page)

-   ****Trending Now Section:****
-   -   ****Section Header:**** Title text "Trending now" positioned top-left.
    -   ****Horizontal Carousel:**** A scrollable horizontal row of poster thumbnail cards.
    -   ****Card Data:**** Each card features a large white-outline number (1, 2, 3, etc.) indicating rank, overlaid on a poster image containing the title of the media.
    -   ****Navigation:**** A right-facing chevron arrow indicating the carousel is scrollable.
-   ****"More reasons to join" Section (Value Proposition):****
-   -   ****Section Header:**** Title text "More reasons to join".
    -   ****Feature Grid:**** Four dark-grey, rectangular cards placed in a row.
    -   ****Card 1 (Enjoy on your TV):**** Explains device compatibility (smart TVs, PlayStation, Xbox, Chromecast, Apple TV, Blu-ray).
    -   ****Card 2 (Download your series):**** Explains the ability to save favorites for offline viewing.
    -   ****Card 3 (Watch everywhere):**** Explains streaming capability across mobile, tablet, laptop, and TV.
    -   ****Card 4 (Create profiles for children):**** Explains custom kid profiles with access to child-friendly content.

### 4\. FAQ Accordion Section

-   ****Section Header:**** Prominent white text "Frequently Asked Questions".
-   ****Question List:**** A vertical stack of dark-grey rectangular bars.
-   ****Interactive Element:**** Each bar contains a text-based question and a `+` (plus) symbol aligned to the far right edge, indicating an accordion-style dropdown.
-   ****Questions Found:****
-   -   "What is Netflix?"
    -   "How much does Netflix cost?"
    -   "Where can I watch?"
    -   "How do I cancel?"
    -   "What can I watch on Netflix?"
    -   "Is Netflix good for children?"

### 5\. Persistent Call to Action & Fine Print

-   ****Secondary CTA Block:**** Positioned directly below the FAQ section. It repeats the initial landing page CTA but with a shorter prompt.
-   ****Text:**** "Ready to watch? Enter your email address to start your membership."
-   ****Form:**** Same Email address input field and "Try 30 days for 0 ₫ >" red button.
-   ****Legal Fine Print:**** A paragraph of centered, tiny gray text outlining the limitations of the offer (e.g., "This offer is only valid for new members...", "will automatically continue your membership until you cancel...", "payment methods may not be eligible...").

### 6\. Global Footer (Bottom Navigation)

-   ****Footer Header:**** A left-aligned link reading "Questions? Contact us."
-   ****Link Column Navigation:**** A 4-column grid of text-based hyperlinks.
-   -   __Column 1:__ FAQ, Investor Relations, Privacy, Speed Test.
    -   __Column 2:__ Help Centre, Jobs, Cookie Preferences, Legal Notices.
    -   __Column 3:__ Account, Ways to Watch, Corporate Information, Only on Netflix.
    -   __Column 4:__ Media Centre, Terms of Use, Contact Us.
-   ****Language Selector:**** A secondary globe icon + language dropdown at the bottom left of the link grid (e.g., "English").
-   ****Localized Footer Marker:**** Text showing the localized region (e.g., "Netflix Vietnam").
-   ****Anti-Bot Security Notice:**** A final line of text at the very bottom far left reading "This page is protected by Google reCAPTCHA to ensure you're not a bot."
